# PIOTW Value Bridge v1

The required chain is `PUBLIC FACT → DERIVED INPUT → ASSUMPTION → SCENARIO OUTPUT`.

## Rules

- Published financials are denominators, not the originating operational thesis.
- Each range shows its formula, accounting basis, source and uncertainty.
- Overlapping opportunities are not added.
- Cash release, revenue opportunity and EBITDA are kept separate.
- Outputs are labelled `ILLUSTRATIVE VALUE SCENARIO`, never company guidance.

## Travis Perkins v1 demonstrations

| Opportunity | Public fact / derived input | Assumption | Scenario output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Estate productivity | Two larger relocated branches plus two planning/compliance cases | 2–5% opportunity on a £460m proxy addressable branch-cost pool | £8m–£23m annual EBITDA equivalent |
| Process/service recovery | Oracle-related direct-sales and invoice friction; £4.607bn published Group revenue denominator | 0.2–0.4% revenue-equivalent benefit with simplified conversion | £9m–£18m annual EBITDA scenario |
| Commercial replication | Two public awards and one named managed-service case | No extrapolated win rate; bounded exploratory range | £5m–£15m revenue, no EBITDA estimate |

These are interface scenarios. The £460m cost pool is an explicit proxy, not a reported figure. The ranges overlap and must not be summed.
