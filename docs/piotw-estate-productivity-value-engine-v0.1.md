# PIOTW Value Engine v0.1 — Estate Productivity

## Finding

Merchanting contracted from 769 to 724 branches in 2024 (six openings, 51 closures; -5.85%), then moved to 727 in 2025 (+0.41%). The eligible 2025 peer median was +2.53%. Meanwhile the coarse revenue-per-branch proxy moved from £5.50m in 2022 to £5.12m in 2025.

The measured tension is therefore not simply “too many branches.” It is: a concentrated portfolio reset was followed by stabilisation without an observable recovery in revenue per location. This supports branch-level triage and format-productivity diligence.

## Important boundary

The 209 branches identified by the issuer's impairment review are not assumed to be loss-making. Accounting impairment can reflect cyclically depressed forecasts while a branch remains cash-contributive or strategically important.

## Intervention hypothesis

Segment the estate using branch contribution, local share, service radius, format, customer-transfer risk and property optionality. Choose invest, relocate, consolidate or retain based on that evidence rather than a blanket closure target.

## Value bridge

Published property cash proceeds were £62m and £51m in the two latest years, averaging £56.5m. Low/base/high continuation scenarios assume 50%/75%/100% recurrence: £28m/£42m/£57m gross cash proceeds.

This is not incremental value, EBITDA, profit or guidance. EBITDA is withheld because public evidence lacks comparable occupancy cost, branch contribution and customer-retention data.

## Falsification

The hypothesis weakens if branch-level evidence shows the impaired population is healthy through-cycle, new formats do not outperform, the peer gap disappears after format/geography controls, or disposal value is offset by lost service and new lease costs.
