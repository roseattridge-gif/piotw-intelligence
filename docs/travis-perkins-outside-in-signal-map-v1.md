## Acquisition rule

Every record must retain source family, URL, title, publication/effective dates, legal or trading entity, business unit, exact evidence span, source confidence and immutable hash. Business-unit evidence is never silently promoted to Group evidence.

## Real features built

The v1 lab computes seven bounded development features: estate modernisation, estate/planning complexity, public awards, specialist leadership redesign, technology execution, managed-service productivity evidence and certified-timber movement. Each feature lists its source IDs. Careers, complaint volumes, supplier concentration and complete branch history remain unavailable.
