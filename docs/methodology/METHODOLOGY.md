# Methodology

Facts are directly supported observations. Signals are deterministic transformations of facts. Hypotheses combine signed evidence into a probability plus a separate evidence-confidence measure. Predictions are timestamped forward-looking records. Contribution equals bounded strength × reliability × materiality × family-specific recency × independence × relevance. Common-source clusters reduce independence; every component is retained so results reconstruct exactly. Language models may extract candidate facts and explanations but never select final scores.
