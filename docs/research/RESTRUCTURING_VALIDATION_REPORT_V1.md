# Restructuring validation report v1

Resolved: 13 August 2026
Scope: 10 companies, two historical cutoffs, 20 immutable prediction occasions

## Result

Four previously unannounced qualifying restructuring programmes were found inside the 12-month windows: Elementis, James Cropper, Ricardo and Spirax Group, all after the 31 December 2022 cutoff. Sixteen occasions were resolved negative under the protocol's exclusion rules.

The PIOTW rules model achieved a Brier score of **0.137896**, compared with **0.166400** for the frozen 12% constant prior. Its ROC AUC was **0.75**, average precision **0.590476**, and highest-risk-quintile lift **2.5x**. Median warning time across the four positive outcomes was **165.5 days**.

At the frozen 0.5 classification threshold the model predicted no positives and therefore missed all four events. The result shows useful ranking and probability information in this small sample, but it is not an operational alert classifier at that threshold.

## Gate decision

**The formal validation gate is not passed.** The model met the numerical candidate condition against the fully frozen prior and showed useful ranking and lead time. However, the protocol declared two simple-rule baselines without freezing their numerical thresholds or probability mapping before outcome review. Their post-outcome results are sensitivity analyses and cannot be used to certify the gate.

Do not claim statistical significance, validated proprietary intelligence, calibrated production probabilities or commercial superiority. Before expansion, freeze the complete numeric baseline definitions, independently adjudicate the 20 labels, and run a genuinely untouched temporal holdout.

## Outcome audit

The four positives were:

- Elementis — Fit for the Future, announced in September 2023; month-only timing represented as 30 September.
- James Cropper — Paper division capacity and redundancy restructuring, announced 19 April 2023.
- Ricardo — accelerated Automotive and Industrial restructuring, announced 21 February 2023.
- Spirax Group — Watson-Marlow manufacturing-capacity and overhead restructuring, announced 10 August 2023.

The outcome ledger is `data/restructuring/outcomes.csv`. It records the source, resolution note, exclusion applied and review status for every occasion. Already-public programmes were not relabelled as new events merely because charges or implementation continued after cutoff.

## Deviations and limitations

1. Financial-stress and disclosure-language thresholds were formalised after outcome inspection; those comparisons are sensitivity only.
2. One researcher resolved the outcomes. There was no independent blinded second adjudicator.
3. The pre-cutoff evidence hash proves the structured extraction, URL, features and exclusions used by the model. It is not a hash of a locally preserved full source document.
4. Twenty occasions across ten companies are a feasibility sample, not a statistically powered validation set.

The reproducible result is generated by `scripts/evaluate_restructuring_validation.py` and written to `data/derived/restructuring_validation_results.json`; the SQLite outcome rows reject updates and deletes.
