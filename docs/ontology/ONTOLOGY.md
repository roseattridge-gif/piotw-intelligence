# Operational ontology v0.1

Each condition is a competing hypothesis, not a diagnosis. Its configuration records candidate supporting signals, contradictions, alternative explanations, applicable sectors, interventions and objective outcome labels.

| Condition | Candidate support | Candidate contradiction / alternative | Candidate outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supply constraint | inventory/revenue divergence; delivery language; supplier hiring | demand fall; deliberate build; sector inflation | supplier remediation; inventory correction |
| Quality deterioration | warranty/provision rise; quality hiring; rework language | product-mix or reporting change | remediation; margin deterioration |
| Production constraint | backlog/output gap; utilisation language | strategic capacity reservation | capacity investment; missed delivery |
| Forecasting weakness | repeated guidance changes; inventory volatility | macro shock | planning/data programme |
| Working-capital pressure | inventory/receivables growth; cash conversion fall | acquisition or growth investment | inventory correction; restructuring |
| Cost-base inflation | margin compression with flat volume | temporary input-price shock | cost programme |
| Underutilisation | fixed-cost absorption; falling volume | planned maintenance | site consolidation |
| Labour constraint | vacancies; attrition; wage pressure | planned expansion | hiring programme; automation |
| Engineering bottleneck | engineer vacancies; launch delay | deliberate R&D investment | programme delay; capacity investment |
| Digital debt | ERP fragmentation; manual-control language | controlled migration | ERP/data programme |
| Transformation drag | repeated reset; exceptional cost without benefit | phased long-term programme | transformation relaunch |
| Customer friction | complaints; returns; service backlog | isolated product event | service remediation |
| Commercial weakness | order decline; discounting; lost contracts | deliberate market exit | profit warning |
| Procurement weakness | supplier concentration; input variance | commodity-wide shock | sourcing programme |
| Knowledge concentration | key-person dependence; succession gaps | specialist operating model | leadership/talent intervention |
| Leadership instability | repeated senior changes | planned succession | senior leadership change |
| Acquisition integration risk | synergies delayed; systems duplication | planned integration curve | impairment; restructuring |
| Capacity constraint | capex urgency; wait times; backlog | speculative expansion | capacity investment |
| Demand weakness | orders/book-to-bill decline | capacity-limited sales | margin deterioration |
| Execution recovery | cash conversion, delivery and margin improve | base effect; sector rebound | margin recovery |
