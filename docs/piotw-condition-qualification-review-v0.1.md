## Procurement entity-resolution pilot

The live store contains repeated names for Galaxy Cars (4 records), Pearson Professional Assessments Limited (2) and Convergence Group (2). Records cluster within hours, may be duplicate/amended notices, and contain no company registration identifier or approved parent relationship. Name recognition alone cannot attach them to a PIOTW company.

No supplier mapping was approved and no procurement condition entered runtime. A safe next attempt needs primary legal-entity evidence, stable notice deduplication and an explicit parent/subsidiary relationship where relevant.

## Corroboration

The contract distinguishes repeated observations in one source, multiple observations within one family, independent source families, derivative duplicates and contradiction. Current careers evidence is `MULTIPLE_OBSERVATIONS_ONE_FAMILY`; real cross-source corroboration does not yet exist.

## Conclusion

The policy behaves sensibly. Evidence depth, not an obviously wrong threshold, is binding. Calibration should wait for at least four healthy snapshots and more than one rich snapshot per company. Compare should not begin from these unqualified candidates.
