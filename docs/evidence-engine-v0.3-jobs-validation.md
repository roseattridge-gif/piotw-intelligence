# Evidence Engine 0.3 - jobs validation

The trial requires at least three healthy snapshots and 100 independently labelled postings. A job closes only after two consecutive healthy misses. Fetch failure, source outage, or an anomalous site-wide drop suppresses closure. Reappearing title/location signatures are linked as possible reposts, not silently counted as new facts.

The independent label schema covers function, seniority, normalized location, duplicate/repost, and lifecycle status. The current schedule establishes baseline and two elapsed-time follow-ups. Until those snapshots and labels exist, discovery recall, classification accuracy, and false-closure rate are unmeasured.
