# PIOTW Estate Peer Cohort v1

Status: development benchmark, not validated.

## Target

Travis Perkins Merchanting, not the consolidated Group or Toolstation. The measured unit combines national general merchanting and specialist merchant formats.

## Inclusion principles

Peers must operate a UK trade-facing branch/depot distribution model, publish an observable location count, and disclose comparable period movement. Revenue mix, geography and format differences remain explicit.

Peers were selected before calculating the target position. No peer was added or removed to improve Travis Perkins' rank.

## Frozen comparability rule

A feature needs the target plus at least three peers with the same geography, period, unit and count definition. Missing is not zero. An inferred opening bridge must be explicitly labelled. Revenue per location is withheld because formats, manufacturing exposure and product economics are materially unlike.
